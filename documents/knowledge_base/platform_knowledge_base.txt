Enterprise AI Platform — Business Intelligence Knowledge Base
============================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
The Enterprise AI Decision Intelligence Platform provides real-time business
intelligence through machine learning models, an AI assistant, and interactive
dashboards. It covers four core analytical domains: fraud detection, customer
churn prediction, sales forecasting, and customer segmentation.

FRAUD DETECTION
---------------
The fraud detection model scores every financial transaction in real time.
It uses a Random Forest or XGBoost classifier trained on nine transaction
features: amount, hour of day, day of week, weekend flag, night-time flag,
amount-vs-customer-average ratio, high-amount flag, payment method risk score,
and unknown device flag.

Risk levels:
- LOW      (probability < 0.40): normal transaction, no action required.
- MEDIUM   (0.40 – 0.60):        flag for manual review.
- HIGH     (0.60 – 0.80):        escalate to fraud team.
- CRITICAL (>= 0.80):            block transaction immediately.

Payment method risk scores: credit card = 1, debit card = 1,
bank transfer = 1, PayPal = 2, cash = 3, crypto = 4.

CUSTOMER CHURN PREDICTION
--------------------------
The churn model predicts the probability that a customer will stop purchasing
within the next 90 days. Features include account age, total transactions,
total spend, average order value, days since last purchase, support ticket
count, open tickets, purchase frequency, average monthly spend, high-ticket
flag, and 90-day inactivity flag.

Customers with churn probability >= 0.60 are classified HIGH or CRITICAL risk
and should receive proactive retention outreach.

SALES FORECASTING
-----------------
The sales forecasting model uses a Gradient Boosting Regressor with recursive
multi-step prediction. It is trained on daily revenue data with lag features
(7-day, 14-day, 30-day lags), rolling averages, and calendar features
(month, day of week, week of year, quarter).

The recursive approach: each predicted day becomes the lag input for the next
day, allowing genuine multi-horizon forecasts without data leakage.

CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION
---------------------
Customers are grouped into five segments using KMeans clustering:
- High Value:     Top revenue contributors, frequent buyers, long tenure.
- Loyal:          Consistent purchasers with strong recent activity.
- At Risk:        Previously active customers now showing disengagement.
- New Customer:   Recently acquired, still in onboarding phase.
- Low Engagement: Minimal activity, low purchase frequency.

DATA PRIVACY
------------
All customer data is stored in encrypted MySQL databases.
Personal data is processed in accordance with GDPR and applicable regulations.
Data retention: transaction records are kept for 7 years; behavioural data
for 3 years. Customers may request data deletion via the support portal.

API USAGE LIMITS
----------------

Authentication endpoints: 20 login attempts per minute per IP.
Registration: 10 registrations per hour per IP.
Maximum request body size: 1 MB.
